About & Guidelines

Please read these before posting.

  • ruleCommunity Rules

    1. Share real experiences.
    2. Do not post names or addresses of private people.
    3. No threats or hate speech.
    4. Keep posts about your local community.
    5. Slurs, hate speech, and name-calling are automatically screened - posts that break this rule can't be submitted.

  • lockPrivacy

    No account or email is ever needed, and posting anonymously is the default. If you choose to show a name, only the name you type is displayed - it is not linked to you or your device. Before you post, remove anything that could identify you or someone else - addresses, phone numbers, or photos that show faces.

  • flagReporting Posts

    If a post shares personal information, spreads false claims, or harasses someone, tap Report Post and pick a reason. Here is what happens next:

    1. Your report is sent anonymously.
    2. A moderator reviews it, usually within 24 hours.
    3. Posts that break the rules are removed.
    4. The poster is never told who reported them.

  • health_and_safetySafety Tips

    If someone is in danger, call 911. Use a private browser window on shared devices.

Our Mission

Give people a safe way to speak up about problems in their community without fear of retaliation.
