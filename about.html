About & Guidelines

Please read these before posting.

  • ruleCommunity Rules

    1. Share real experiences.
    2. Do not post names or addresses of private people.
    3. No threats or hate speech.
    4. Keep posts about your local community or state.
    5. A basic word filter blocks listed slurs and warns about some name-calling. It can miss harmful content or flag words out of context, so it does not replace human moderation.

  • lockPrivacy

    No account or email is required by this prototype. Anonymous is the default; a display name is optional. A display name is not verified, and this prototype cannot guarantee anonymity. Draft text and any display name are saved in this browser without encryption. Encrypted drafts and additional anonymity tools remain planned. Avoid identifying details and do not use this prototype for sensitive whistleblowing.

  • flagReporting Posts

    If a post shares personal information, spreads false claims, or harasses someone, tap Report Post and pick a reason. This prototype demonstrates the form; it does not send flags to a moderator yet.

    The planned live process is to collect a flag, have a moderator review it against the rules, and remove content that breaks those rules. Reporter details should not be shared with the poster. No review turnaround time is promised.

  • health_and_safetySafety Tips

    If someone is in danger, call 911. Use a private browser window on shared devices.

Our Mission

Help people bring attention to injustice, discrimination, harmful behavior, and troubling events in their local or state communities. Anonymous sharing is the goal; real privacy protections and moderation are needed before public launch.
